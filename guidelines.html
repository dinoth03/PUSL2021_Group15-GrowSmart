GrowSmart User Guidelines

🌱 1. Getting Started

Create an Account: Sign up using a valid email address to unlock all features of the GrowSmart platform.

Login: Access your dashboard by logging in with your credentials.

Profile Setup: Complete your profile by adding personal details and gardening preferences to receive tailored suggestions.

🛒 2. Exploring the Shop

Browse Categories: Navigate through House Plants, Fruits & Vegetables, and Flowers under the "Plant" menu.

Product Details: View detailed descriptions, prices, and images before adding items to your cart.

Checkout: Use the secure checkout process to complete purchases. Track your order status via your account dashboard.

🤖 3. Smart Gardening with AI

AI Chatbot: Access the AI assistant from the "Chat" menu for real-time plant care tips, pest solutions, and more.

Learning Resources: Visit the “Learning” section under "More" to expand your knowledge on gardening practices.

Idea & Inspiration: Get design inspiration and creative gardening ideas to enhance your green space.

🌿 4. Sustainable Gardening

Sustainability Garden: Learn how to build a self-sustaining garden using eco-friendly methods.

Green Tips: Use compost, reduce water waste, and adopt organic techniques.

🎉 5. Community & Competitions

Join the Community: Interact with fellow gardeners, ask questions, and share your plant journey.

Competitions: Participate in seasonal contests and win exciting prizes by showcasing your garden.

Points & Promotions: Earn points and explore promotional offers from GrowSmart.

⚠️ 6. Guidelines & Conduct

Be Respectful: Foster a friendly environment. Respect all users and their contributions.

No Spam: Avoid fake listings or misleading content. All listings must follow GrowSmart's policies.

Report Abuse: Use the reporting system to flag inappropriate behavior or content.

🔒 7. Privacy & Security

Data Protection: Keep your login credentials secure. Do not share personal information publicly.

Secure Payments: Only use GrowSmart’s recommended payment channels to avoid scams.

Privacy Settings: Manage your account settings to control what information is visible to others.
